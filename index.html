FrenzyCat Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

In short: FrenzyCat has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your cat's profile, photo and play history stay on your device. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

1. Who we are

FrenzyCat is published by Ömer Edip Aras. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at omerediparas@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your cat's profile: name, age band, accent color and optional photo
  • Play session history and statistics
  • Your settings, such as reminder and privacy choices, and the result of the optional favorite-prey test

This information is not sent to us or to any server. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset all data, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Camera and photos

If you choose to add a photo of your cat, the app asks to use your camera or lets you pick a picture from your photo library. The picture is reduced in size and saved inside the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded. The app does not access any other photos, and you can remove the photo in the cat's profile at any time.

4. Usage statistics

The app includes an optional setting for anonymous usage statistics. In the current version, no usage data is transmitted off your device. If we ever begin collecting anonymous statistics, we will update this policy first, and the data will never include names or photos.

5. Purchases

FrenzyCat may offer an optional Premium upgrade in the future. Any purchase would be processed by Apple. We would not receive your payment card details.

6. Other things the app does

  • The app keeps your screen awake while a play session is running.
  • The app plays sounds during play.
  • Links, such as this policy, open in your web browser, which is governed by the website's own policy.

7. Third parties

FrenzyCat does not include third-party advertising or analytics services. If you install the app through Apple's TestFlight or App Store, Apple handles that distribution under Apple's Privacy Policy.

8. Children

FrenzyCat is made for cat owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your choices and rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it: you can view, change or delete it inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling. If you believe we hold personal information about you, email us and we will respond and, where applicable, delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example if Premium purchases or analytics are added. The effective date at the top will change, and significant changes will be announced in the app.
